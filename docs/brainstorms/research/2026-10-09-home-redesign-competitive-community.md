# Home redesign — competitive & community patterns

## What effective training homes showcase

| Pattern | Who | Steal for Fathom | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly streak at top of Home | **MySwimPro** ([support](https://support.myswimpro.com/en/articles/17252645-your-weekly-streak)) | Streak + this-week progress (done/target) as habit identity | Hard reset with no forgiveness (Fathom already chose floor + Rest Passes) |
| Workout / plan next to Home | **MySwimPro** — WOD on Coach; plan dashboard expected on Home ([Reddit: plan disappeared](https://www.reddit.com/r/myswimpro/comments/14lg9rw/my_swimming_plan_is_no_longer_appearing/)) | Put **today’s course** on Home (community expects plan + WOD visible) | Burying active plan behind another tab |
| Personal status before social | **Strava** — streak / you before feed ([UX Snaps](https://www.uxsnaps.com/fitness-activity-feed)) | Sole-athlete: personal progress + chore first | Social feed on Fathom Home |
| Content-aware streak strip | Strava (activity icons per day) | Week-adherence dots tied to planned sessions | Generic vanity ticks for app opens |
| Large “today’s run” / guided card | **Nike Run Club** | Primary CTA card for today’s training | Overstuffed marketing cards / feature promos crowding the action |
| Widgets for streak / volume | MySwimPro iOS widgets | Later: OS widgets; for now in-app adherence + streak | Requiring widgets for the habit loop |
| Community frustration: forced streak chrome | [Strava community](https://communityhub.strava.com/archived-strava-features-chat-5/new-strava-ui-layout-on-to-of-the-feed-in-the-home-tab-10870) | Keep streak glanceable; allow future hide-game-layer | Un-hideable noisy banners |

## Community / logging takeaways

- Swimmers care that the **active plan and next workout stay visible**; losing the plan dashboard is a support-class failure (MySwimPro Reddit).
- Serious athletes tolerate rest days; **weekly** consistency beats daily streak pressure (aligned with MySwimPro / Strava / NRC and Fathom Programs research).
- Logging credibility: Home should surface **attributable** sleep + sessions (instrument honesty) while the game layer stays **forgiving** — same split as Programs gamification brief.

## Sprint / strength apps (directional)

- Sprint/speed apps and strength plan apps typically hero **today’s prescribed session** and a **block progress** meter, not a vitals dashboard. Fathom’s hybrid (progress strip + chore CTA) matches that pattern and extends cleanly when running/strength Agons land.

## Logging research (brief)

- Prefer **informational** progress feedback over controlling rewards (SDT / CHI 2026 Duolingo rewards — see `2026-10-09-home-reward-dopamine-gamification.md`).
- Progress Principle: make today’s inch of meaningful training progress visible on open.
- Do not log or reward “app open”; log chore attempt / session sync / weekly floor.
