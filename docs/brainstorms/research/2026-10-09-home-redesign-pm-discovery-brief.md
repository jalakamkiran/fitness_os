# Home redesign — PM discovery brief

## Problem framing (MITRE-style, dogfood)

**Look inward**
- Symptom: Home is skipped; swim history is the real entry.
- Bias risk: “industry standard Home” can become a KPI dashboard that still isn’t worth opening.
- We are part of the problem: Programs shipped as a separate surface → extra click; Home stayed instrument-only while Agon gained meaning.

**Look outward**
- Who has it: sole owner-athlete (dogfood). Severity: low open motivation for Home; Programs underused from Home.
- Who doesn’t: multi-user / social athletes (out of scope).
- When: morning open / post-workout sync moments.

**Reframe**
- Problem statement: *Home does not make training progress and today’s next action visible and rewarding enough to be the reason to open Fathom.*
- HMW: *How might we make Home the one-tap place for “how am I progressing?” and “what’s today’s course?” without inventing coach scores or fixing sync in this cut?*

## Opportunity solution tree (compressed)

```
Desired outcome: Owner opens Home willingly; one-tap into today’s training
│
├─ Opportunity: Make progress salient on open
│   └─ Solution: Hybrid hero (streak / Odyssey / Areté–Depth strip)
├─ Opportunity: Remove Programs hop
│   └─ Solution: Chore/proof primary CTA on Home
├─ Opportunity: Keep day truth visible
│   └─ Solution: Sleep + today’s sessions + week-adherence dots
└─ Opportunity: Idle Agon still intentional
    └─ Solution: Day lead + Odyssey snapshot / upsell
```

Non-opportunities this cut: sync blanking fix; social feed; readiness composite.

## Prioritized assumptions (risk × uncertainty)

| # | Assumption | Risk if wrong | How to probe |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Hybrid hero increases voluntary Home opens vs jumping to Workouts | High — whole bet | Dogfood week + open path diary |
| A2 | One-tap chore is enough; deep Programs home still OK | Med | Task test: find today’s course |
| A3 | Week-adherence dots feel like “useful chart/identity” without clutter | Med | Narrative + vibe prototype preference |
| A4 | Odyssey upsell when idle doesn’t feel spammy | Med | Preference test no-Agon state |
| A5 | Demoting calories/steps won’t feel like “losing Home” | Low–Med | Preference A/B vs old strip |

## Validation backlog (cheap → dear)

1. HTML prototype preference (active Agon / no Agon) — **this worktree**
2. 5-day dogfood: force Home-first opens; note whether chore CTA is used
3. Optional: 3 peer swim-athletes react to prototype frames (if available)

## Business / product outcome (dogfood)

- Primary: Home becomes the preferred open surface for day + training pull.
- Secondary: Agon adherence visibility without opening Programs hub first.
