Agon · Pool swim

How your dates work

Dates follow your trials.They move only when a trial isn’t passed, is delayed, or you pause the Agon. Skipping or shortening a session never moves them.
Areté and Streak stay steady.Streak counts a week when you complete at least half your sessions, and Rest passes apply on their own. Areté is a lifetime total and never goes down, so a trial that isn’t passed moves dates, not points.

The Odyssey

Areté · progress points

Areté is how Fathom counts your progress.You earn it for sessions, trials and Agon milestones. It is a lifetime total that only goes up.
Depth and Kleos build on it.Depth is how far along your Odyssey you are. Kleos marks the major milestones you reach on the way. Streak counts consistent weeks.
