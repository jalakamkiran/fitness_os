01 · Design system

Fathom visual language

Pool-night instrument palette, Manrope + IBM Plex Mono, and the navigation contract that keeps Home calm. Tokens live in tokens.css.

Brand positioning

Fathom is an instrument and analyst — a calm companion for session truth, not a loud coach dashboard.

Is

Depth, measure, understanding

Answers “what today has been” with sync chrome plus a fixed Today stack: last-night sleep, activity totals, today body HR (≤1 spark), and multi-sport session cards. Premium, local-first, sport-agnostic.

Is not

Coach theater or AI glow

No purple gradients, crypto chrome, cartoon mascots, or always-on sync drama. Discobolus is the letter T in the wordmark (word height); splash plays him settling into Fathom. Everyday app icon is a plain F.

Colour

Toggle theme (top-right) to review light and dark. Accent mint and amber carry signal; hot and up are reserved for failure and positive deltas.

Jump links, not tabs

Light pills scroll to Charts / Intervals / Findings. Findings jump auto-expands More findings. No sticky multi-tab bar.

Athlete never-list on detail

No enrichment counts, Sync lines, Type: 6, P0 naming, Evidence chrome, metric footers, Samples dumps, Dev columns, or Run reasoner / Qwen3 UI.

Navigation contract

Frozen tab ownership. Home never borrows analytics depth from Vitals or Workouts.

Tab Owns Does not own
Home Today instrument: sync/pairing chrome, sleep strip, totals, today HR (± one tiny spark), today’s session cards Hypnogram, workout charts, history calendars, diagnosis lists, reasoner settings, food, composite scores
Vitals Physiological trends and history depth Session analysis, workout lists
Workouts Session history + analysis (sport tabs; Swim first) Live connection theater, vitals trend charts

Home never-list

Lock from 2026-09-12 Today instrument. If it needs scroll-depth or interpretation theater, it belongs elsewhere. At most one tiny today-HR spark is allowed.

Logo production constraints

Design Head must-fixes. Full artwork review lives on the logo page.

Wordmark at 16–24px

Primary wordmark must remain identifiable at 16–24px height with no legibility loss. Minimum reproduction: --wordmark-min: 20px (UI chrome); absolute floor --mark-min: 16px.

Single-colour vector only

SVG/PDF artwork only. No gradients, shadows, or fine interior detail. Colour applied as a flat fill from the token palette (ink or accent).

Clear-space

Minimum clear-space around wordmark and mark: --clear-space: 0.35em (measured from capital height). No competing UI or figure in that band.

Discobolus = the T; icon = F — LOCKED

Owner asset replaces the letter T on the Fa/hom line (light spacing a·mark·h). Splash animates figure → letter slot → Fa/hom assemble. App icon is bold F only — never the athlete. Canonical: logo.html.
