Fathom mark

Locked. The Discobolus (Myron’s discus thrower) is the letter T — arm + disc form the crossbar, body the stem — sized to the Fa/hom line with light spacing beside the mark. Everyday icon is a plain F. Splash plays the figure settling into the word.

Primary wordmark

Fa + Discobolus-as-T + hom. Single-colour vector. At small sizes the athlete still reads as a T (crossbar + stem).

Locked Discobolus is the T on the Fa/hom line (spaced a·mark·h). App icon stays F. Do not reopen without owner ask.

App icon — letter F

Everyday icon is a bold F (not the athlete). Keeps splash figure special and chrome legible at 29–60px. Optional later: a sports combo mark — not required for this cut.

Size ladder

Wordmark with athlete-T from 16px up. Icon F stays crisp at favicon sizes.

Clear-space

Minimum clear-space = 0.35em of capital height. Athlete-T stays inside the word — do not pull the figure out as a separate badge in UI.
